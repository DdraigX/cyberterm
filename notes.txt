Basic Color Codes:

Background Colors:
Black: \033[40m
Red: \033[41m
Green: \033[42m
Yellow: \033[43m
Blue: \033[44m
Magenta: \033[45m
Cyan: \033[46m
White: \033[47m

Foreground (Text) Colors:
Black: \033[30m
Red: \033[31m
Green: \033[32m
Yellow: \033[33m
Blue: \033[34m
Magenta: \033[35m
Cyan: \033[36m
White: \033[37m


Reset Color: \033[0m (resets to default terminal colors)
